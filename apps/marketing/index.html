Not just another vibe-coding platform

Most AI builders stop at the prototype. Authenik8 starts at the product.

Vibe-coding tools can generate a screen fast. But real apps need users, permissions, sessions, audit trails, and a backend you can actually own and ship.

The problem

Prototypes break at “add users”

AI-generated demos often skip the hard parts: authentication, server-side sessions, role-based access, and audit history. You get a pretty front end, then rebuild the foundation.

The Authenik8 difference

Identity is in the backend from prompt one

Authenik8 scaffolds real users, rotating sessions, roles, and audit trails into the generated app — not as a mock login screen, but as backend infrastructure you own.

Ownership

Local-first, BYOK, exportable

Build on your machine, bring your own model key, keep files local, and export or deploy when ready. No telemetry, no lock-in, no hosted workspace required to start.
