The problem with vibe-coding platforms

You get a working app — and nothing you can actually deploy with confidence.

01

The app works until someone signs up.

AI builders produce a working prototype — then the moment you need real users, you're building the auth from scratch.

02

No one owns the backend.

Your code is your data model, your sessions, your audit trail — all trapped in someone else's platform.

03

Export is not an export.

You get a zip of files that can't be deployed, audited, or secured the way your product needs.

04

Your key, your bill, your silence.

BYOK is a label on a hosted service. You pay for inference, you don't see anything, and nobody knows what you built.

Why it's different from vibe-coding platforms

A real app, not a prototype you'd trust.

Real users from the first build

Studio scaffolds real auth: rotating JWTs, server-side sessions, roles, and audit events. Not a login form stub — actual identity infrastructure.

Identity is a backend you own

Users, sessions, RBAC, and audit are part of the generated app. Your product has a data model, not a feature tacked on by an AI.

Export that actually deploys

One-click Vercel and Netlify deploys, GitHub sync, ZIP export. Your code is finished and finished-safe — nothing is locked into Studio.

BYOK, zero storage

Your OpenRouter key lives in this browser tab only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. No telemetry, no account needed, nothing to cancel.
